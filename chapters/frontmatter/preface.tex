\chapter{Preface}

This book collects the mathematics and statistics from my undergraduate studies at The University of Queensland into one reference. It is written for relearning quickly. It is more formal than lecture notes, but less formal than a textbook.

\section*{How to use this book}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The book is organised by topic, not by course. The box at the start of each chapter lists the courses its material comes from and the earlier chapters it builds on.
  \item Each chapter is self-contained, apart from what it explicitly builds on.
  \item Definitions, theorems, lemmas, examples and remarks share one counter per chapter, so \emph{Theorem~4.7} comes directly after \emph{Definition~4.6}.
  \item All notation is listed in \cref{app:notation}.
\end{itemize}
