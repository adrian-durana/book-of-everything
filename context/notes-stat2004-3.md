### Suspected errors in PE (kept in place; also listed in the inventory)
1. §1.3: the chi-squared definition sums $n$ terms but writes $\ChiSq k$ ("$n$ degrees of freedom" in the text). The theorem uses lower-case $x_j$ inside a distributional statement (should be $X_j$).
2. §3 water-bottle example: the model is first $\Normal(\mu, 0.1)$, then $\Normal(\mu, 0.01)$.
3. §3.1 uniform example: "The MLE is $\hat\theta = x_{(1)}$"; it is $x_{(n)}$ (the likelihood $\theta^{-n}$ is decreasing on $[x_{(n)},\infty)$). The lead-in says the likelihood is "not bounded (above)"; the point is rather that the maximum is not at a stationary point (the likelihood is discontinuous at $x_{(n)}$).
4. §3.2: regularity condition 4 uses $I(\theta)$ before it is defined; observed and expected information share the symbol $I(\theta)$ (book needs two symbols).
5. §3.2 CRLB proof: Cauchy–Schwarz is written as $(g')^2 = \Cov(T,S)^2\ \ge\ \Var T\,\Var S$; the inequality is $\le$, which is what gives $\Var T\ge (g')^2/I$.
6. §3.3 MLE theorem proof: "take the expectation" of the score equation at $\hat\theta$ mixes the random and fixed arguments; the valid step is $\E_\theta[\text{score}] = 0$ for every $\theta$, then evaluate at $\hat\theta$. The final step divides by $c'(\theta)$ silently. Also the step "$\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\log a(\hat\theta) = \frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}c(\theta)\E[T]$" mixes $\theta$ and $\hat\theta$.
7. §4.1 completeness: "$g(T) = 0$" should be "$\Prob_\theta(g(T)=0) = 1$ for all $\theta$".
8. §4.1 Bernoulli example: "sufficient but not minimal, *as it is not complete*" — incompleteness does not imply non-minimality (only complete + sufficient ⇒ minimal). And $T = (V)_1+(V)_2 = X_n + \frac1{n-1}\sum_{j<n}X_j$ should be $X_n+\sum_{j<n}X_j$.
9. §4.2 Rao–Blackwell proof: $\E[\E[U(\vect X\given T(\vect X))]]$ has a misplaced bracket ($\E[\E[U(\vect X)\given T(\vect X)]]$); "Var[W(T)] ≤ Var[U], with equality if $U = W(T)$" is "iff". Lehmann–Scheffé proof uses completeness without saying so.
10. §5.1 proof: the second inequality keeps the region $\abs{T_n-g}\ge\varepsilon$ but the next line equates the integral to the MSE (integral over the whole space). Minor.
11. §5.2: "leads to some simple methods for computing estimators" — ARE is for comparing estimators. "$T_i\sim\Normal(g(\theta),\tau_i^2/n)$" is approximate.
